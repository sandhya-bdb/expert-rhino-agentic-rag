Supreme Court of India Order (April 26 & 28, 2023) - Eco-Sensitive Zones (ESZ) Modification
Case: In Re: T.N. Godavarman Thirumulpad vs. Union of India & Ors.

RULING & DIRECTIVES:
1. Modification of June 2022 Order: The Supreme Court modified its earlier directive of a mandatory minimum 1-km Eco-Sensitive Zone (ESZ) for all protected areas.
2. Recognition of Local Difficulties: The court recognized that a uniform 1-km ESZ would lead to severe hardships for local citizens living in communities, cities, and towns directly adjacent to national parks (where schools, clinics, and infrastructure already exist).
3. Site-Specific Proposals Allowed: The Supreme Court ruled that the Eco-Sensitive Zone does not have to be a uniform 1-km or 10-km blanket. Instead, state governments are authorized to submit site-specific proposals to the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) for declaring ESZs based on local ecological, geographical, and human needs.
4. Final Approval: Once the state government submits its site-specific proposal and it is reviewed, the Central Government (MoEFCC) notifies the official, customized ESZ for that specific park.

IMPLICATION FOR KAZIRANGA & ASSAM GOVT:
This modification is the direct legal justification used by the Assam Government in 2026. Rather than enforcing the default 10-km zone or a uniform buffer, the state cabinet proposed a site-specific ESZ for Kaziranga National Park with a minimum width of 1 km at certain boundaries (specifically near towns like Bokakhat and Kaliabor) to free local civic areas from strict environmental restrictions, while planning to maintain wider zones elsewhere.
